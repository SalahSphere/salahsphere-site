Our promise

No adverts. No tracking.
No account. No subscription.

Salah is between you and Allah. The app that helps you with it should take nothing from you in return: not your attention, not your data, and not a payment every month.

No adverts
Not now, not ever. Nothing on the screen is trying to sell you anything, least of all during salah.
No tracking
No analytics, and nothing sold or shared. The app doesn't record how you use it, and neither does this website: no cookies, no tracking.
No account
You never sign up or sign in. There is no Salah Sphere server, so there is nothing of yours on one.
No subscription
Free to download. Premium is one payment of £9.99, or £6.99 this Ramadan, and it's yours for life, on this phone and any new one. Nothing renews.
Your location stays with you
Salah times and the Qibla are worked out on your phone, and your location is never sent to me. The app only goes online for the name of your area, Qur'an recitation and the Qur'an meanings you choose.
Nobody needs to know your business
Reminders never name the salah and never play an adhan. When it's time, your phone quietly says so.
No tracker
It keeps no record of the salahs you pray or miss. That is between you and Allah.

So how does it pay for itself?

Through Premium, paid once. That is the whole business: no adverts to show, no data to sell, nothing to renew. If Salah Sphere helps you, the one-time unlock is what keeps it going.
