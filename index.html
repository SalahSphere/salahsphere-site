Between Fajr and Dhuhr

Seventeen times a day,
you ask to be guided.

Every rak'ah begins with Al-Fatihah, and at its heart the same request: guide us to the straight path. Across the five fard salahs, that is seventeen times between dawn and night. How often do we notice what we are asking for?

17 rak'ahs of fard salah each day, and Al-Fatihah in every one. Do you know every word you are saying?
5 appointed times, set by the movement of the sun, not by your calendar. Which do you protect, and which do you squeeze in?
1 direction, shared by some two billion Muslims, wherever they stand. When did you last feel part of that?

You already pick up your phone countless times a day.
Salah Sphere is built so that five of them lead you back to your Creator.

The last third

For the hours most people sleep through.

Salah Times shows the middle of the night and the start of its last third, for Tahajjud. Guided Salah has Tahajjud and Witr when you rise for them.

In Ramadan, Guided Taraweeh opens: 8 or 20 rak'ahs, reciting the whole Qur'an in order across the month and finishing on the 27th night, with a month plan.

What it doesn't do

Some things are left out on purpose.

An app for salah should know when to stay quiet. These are not missing features; they are decisions.

No adhan
The reminder is a calm sound, a buzz, or nothing at all, and it never names the salah.
No tracker
It keeps no record of the salahs you pray or miss. That is between you and Allah.
No account
Nothing to sign up to and nothing to sign in to. There is no Salah Sphere server.
Not the whole Qur'an
Guided Qur'an holds Al-Fatihah, Juz 'Amma and four more surahs, to read and understand. Keep your mushaf for the rest.
Not the masjid
Guided Salah and Pray behind an imam teach the salah. The masjid and the jama'ah are still the place for it.
A note on subscriptions

Salah should never
have a renewal date.

Somewhere along the way, it became normal to pay every month to be reminded of salah. As if remembering Allah were a pay-monthly service.

I believe that is wrong.

The adhan has called people to salah for fourteen centuries, and it has never sent an invoice. Your salah app shouldn't either.
