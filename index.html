Before you scroll on

Seventeen times a day,
you ask to be guided.

Every rak'ah begins with Al-Fatihah, and at its heart the same request: guide us to the straight path. Across the five obligatory prayers, that is seventeen times between dawn and nightfall. How often do we notice what we are asking for?

17 rak'ahs of obligatory prayer each day, and Al-Fatihah in every one. Do you know every word you are saying?
5 appointed times, set by the movement of the sun, not by your calendar. Which do you protect, and which do you squeeze in?
1 direction, shared by some two billion Muslims, wherever they stand. When did you last feel part of that?

You already pick up your phone countless times a day.
Salah Sphere is built so that five of them lead you back to your Creator.

Inside the app

Everything for the prayer.
Nothing to distract from it.

Knowing when, facing the right way, and praying well, with help that steps back once you no longer need it.

Prayer times

Time is the first thing every prayer asks of you.

Salah Sphere works out each time on your phone for exactly where you are, using the method followed where you live. The only question left is whether you answer.

  • Hanafi or Shafi'i Asr, and fine-tuning to match your local masjid
  • The middle of the night and the last third, for Tahajjud
  • Discreet reminders, never a full adhan in a quiet room
  • A next-prayer widget for your home screen
Guided Prayer

Many of us learned to pray as children, and time has loosened what we knew.

Guided Prayer walks through every position and every word at a pace you can actually follow, with stillness in each posture, so your prayer rests on knowledge rather than habit.

  • Fard, Sunnah and Witr, for brothers and sisters
  • Every word in clear transliteration, phrase by phrase
  • Memorise each part, with its meaning, before you pray
  • Guided Taraweeh across Ramadan
  • Just press play. After that, nothing scrolls and nothing needs a tap
Guided Wudu

Every prayer begins with purity.

Ten illustrated steps, each read aloud in a calm voice, so wet hands never need to touch the screen. Learn the steps first, or simply follow along at the sink.

  • A male or a female guiding voice
  • Gentle timing for every washing
  • The supplication at the end, in full
Guided Qur'an

Do you know what you are saying?

Follow Al-Fatihah and Juz 'Amma word by word, each word with its pronunciation and its meaning, until the surahs you pray stop being sounds you remember and become words you understand.

  • The meaning in 17 languages
  • Six reciters, ayah by ayah
  • Al-Kahf, Ya-Sin, Ar-Rahman and Al-Mulk
Qibla

Wherever you stand, you face the same place.

A live compass that settles calmly on the Qibla and gives a gentle buzz when you are aligned, corrected for the magnetic variation where you are.

Du'as & adhkar

Remember Allah before the world reaches you.

Du'as from the Qur'an and the Sunnah, each with its Arabic, a clear transliteration, its meaning and its source, and the morning and evening adhkar with a counter that keeps your place.
