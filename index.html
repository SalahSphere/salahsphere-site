Pray behind an imam

Know what to do when you join the row.

Practise praying behind an imam before you stand in the masjid. Guided Salah plays every word so you can learn it; this works like a real jama'ah instead. You hear only what an imam says aloud, each takbir and the recitation, and a short spoken cue tells you what to do: where your hands go, when to bow, what to read quietly. What is said quietly isn't played, just as in the masjid; its words are on the screen.

  • The five fard salahs, Hanafi or Shafi'i, for brothers and sisters
  • Practice for home: the masjid and the jama'ah are still the place for it

Salah times and reminders

Worked out on your phone for exactly where you are, with no internet needed. Standard or Hanafi Asr, your own minute changes to match your masjid, the night's last third for Tahajjud, and a next-salah widget for your home screen.

Qibla

A compass that settles on the Qibla and gives two long buzzes when you face it, so you can look up from the screen. In a new city, a friend's house, a quiet corner of a station.

Wudu

Guided Wudu reads each step aloud, in a man's or a woman's voice, with a timer for each washing, so wet hands never need to touch the screen. Practise Wudu shows the ten steps, each with its own picture, to learn at your own pace.

Qur'an & du'as

Seventeen times a day, you ask to be guided.

Every rak'ah has Al-Fatihah, and at its heart the same request: guide us to the straight path. Across the five fard salahs, that is seventeen times between dawn and night. Do you know every word you are saying?

Guided Qur'an

Read and listen to Al-Fatihah and Juz 'Amma ayah by ayah, each with its pronunciation and its meaning, until the surahs you recite stop being sounds you remember and become words you understand. The meaning in 17 languages, six reciters, and Al-Kahf, Ya-Sin, Ar-Rahman and Al-Mulk too.

Du'as & adhkar

Du'as from the Qur'an and the Sunnah, each with its Arabic, a clear transliteration, its meaning and its source, and the morning and evening adhkar ready each day. Search every du'a, keep your own lists, one ready for Umrah and Hajj, and find the du'a of the day on Salah Times.

What it doesn't do

Some things are left out on purpose.

An app for salah should know when to stay quiet. These are not missing features; they are decisions.

No adhan
The reminder is a calm sound, a buzz, or nothing at all, and it never names the salah.
No tracker
It keeps no record of the salahs you pray or miss. That is between you and Allah.
No account
Nothing to sign up to and nothing to sign in to. There is no Salah Sphere server.
No adverts, no tracking
No analytics, and nothing sold or shared. Your location is used on your phone and never sent to me, and a backup goes only where you put it.
Not the whole Qur'an
Guided Qur'an holds Al-Fatihah, Juz 'Amma and four more surahs, to read and understand. Keep your mushaf for the rest.
Not the masjid
Guided Salah and Pray behind an imam teach the salah. The masjid and the jama'ah are still the place for it.
